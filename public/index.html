Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about payment, privacy, family access, records, and troubleshooting.

Showing all questions

What is Lineage?

Lineage is a private family-tree and storytelling application. It helps a family record relatives, relationships, important life events, photographs, sources, and memories in one shared archive.

Who is Lineage for?

It is for individuals, households, clans, and extended families who want an organised record they can build together. You can begin with only a few known relatives and expand over time.

What happens after my account is approved?

The dashboard unlocks when you sign in or refresh your approval status. You can build your first tree, add relatives, invite family members, create timeline events, and preserve stories.

Does every person in the tree need an account?

No. A profile represents a relative in the family record; an account is only needed when someone wants to sign in and participate. Never create shared login credentials for a group.

Why is KES 500 required?

The one-time unlock discourages automated or disposable signups and supports operation of the private family archive. Payment is manually matched to the submitted M-Pesa transaction code before access is approved.

Is KES 500 a subscription?

No. The current access model is a one-time KES 500 account unlock, not a monthly subscription.

How do I pay with M-Pesa?

Choose Send Money in M-Pesa, send exactly KES 500 to 254113245740, then enter the transaction code in the form above. Do not submit your PIN or the full confirmation message.

Can I pay from another person's phone?

Yes. Enter the optional payer phone number with the transaction code so the administrator can match the payment. The Lineage account remains attached to your own email address.

How long does approval take?

Approval is manual, so timing depends on administrator availability and whether the submitted details match the M-Pesa payment. Use “Check approval status” above; you do not need to pay again while a valid submission is pending.

What if my payment submission is rejected?

The rejection reason appears on this screen. Check the transaction code and payer number, then submit the corrected details. A rejection does not require another payment when the original payment was valid.

Will Lineage ever ask for my M-Pesa PIN?

No. Lineage does not need your M-Pesa PIN, email password, banking password, or one-time verification codes. Anyone requesting them is not following the official process.

Do relatives need the tree creator's login details?

No. Every participating relative should use their own account and join through an invitation. Sharing the creator's password removes accountability and puts private family records at risk.

How do I invite relatives?

An owner or administrator creates an invitation for the relative's email address and selects a role. The relative signs in or creates an account through that invitation.

What can viewers, contributors, admins, and owners do?

Viewers read permitted records. Contributors can add family content. Administrators manage access and shared records. Owners have full administration and can transfer ownership. Privacy rules still apply at every role.

Can I belong to more than one family?

Yes. One account can belong to multiple family trees, and you can switch the active family from the dashboard.

What happens if the family owner leaves?

The owner should transfer ownership to another existing member before deleting their account. Lineage prevents deletion when shared families still require an ownership transfer.

Is my family tree public or searchable by strangers?

No. Family records require an approved account and membership in that family. Lineage is not a public people-search directory.

How are living relatives protected?

Living-person controls can limit profile details for lower-access members. The same restrictions apply to timeline events, stories, direct links, and exports so sensitive information is not revealed indirectly.

Can I make a profile private?

Yes. Profile visibility can be restricted, and private content remains subject to creator and administrator controls. Contributors cannot use a private profile they are not allowed to manage.

Are uploaded photographs public?

No. Current media is stored privately and delivered through authenticated family access. A photograph attached to a protected profile is not exposed as a public file.

Does an export include hidden information?

No. Family-tree and archive exports are filtered using the requesting member's permissions. Information they cannot view in the application should not appear in their export.

What should never be stored in Lineage?

Do not store passwords, M-Pesa PINs, banking details, national-ID images, or unnecessary medical and financial information. Ask permission before adding sensitive details or photographs of living people.

Which relationships can I record?

You can record parents, children, spouses, siblings, grandparents, grandchildren, cousins, aunts or uncles, nieces or nephews, and custom relatives.

What can be added to the family timeline?

Timeline events include births, education, marriage, work, migration, milestones, deaths, places, approximate dates, descriptions, and optional source links.

What are family stories?

Stories are long-form memories in the family journal. They can include a date and place, tag relevant relatives, use controlled visibility, and receive comments from permitted family members.

Can mistakes and duplicate people be corrected?

Permitted contributors can edit records. Duplicate detection helps identify repeated people, while merge controls consolidate records. Deleted people can be restored from the recycle bin before permanent removal.

Can I download my records?

Yes. Lineage supports an Excel family-tree export, a privacy-filtered JSON family archive, and a personal account-data export. GEDCOM and printed-book generation are not currently available.

Can I use Lineage on my phone?

Yes. The tree, timeline, stories, account controls, payment screen, and editors are responsive on modern phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

Why must I verify my email before paying?

Email verification protects account recovery and helps ensure payment approval is attached to a reachable account. Use the resend button above if the original message did not arrive.

What should I do if I see “Unauthorized”?

Your session may have expired. Sign in again and refresh the page. If login briefly succeeds and then ends, allow cookies for the site and retry without private-browsing restrictions.

How do I reset a forgotten password?

Use “Forgot your password?” on the sign-in screen. The emailed recovery link is single-use and expires for security; request a new one if necessary.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, from Privacy & account after approval. Shared-family owners must transfer ownership first. Account deletion requires your password and an explicit confirmation phrase.

Does Lineage replace my own backups?

No. Keep copies of irreplaceable photographs and source documents. Use the available exports regularly; automated user-managed backup and GEDCOM workflows are not yet available.

Why is my approval screen still showing after payment?

Payment alone does not unlock the account. Submit the M-Pesa transaction code, wait for manual verification, then use “Check approval status”. Do not submit repeated payments for the same account.
